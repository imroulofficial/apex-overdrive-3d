Privacy Policy

Apex Overdrive: 3D Racing · Red Pen Studio · Effective November 14, 2026 (updated for Meta install measurement and US opt-out choices)

Summary: Apex Overdrive plays fully offline. Your progress is saved on your device first, and a backup copy of your game progress is kept in Google Firebase under a random anonymous player ID, with no name, email or sign-up. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects device data (including your advertising ID) as described below. Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. To measure our ads on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), the Meta SDK reports app installs and app opens to Meta, only with your consent where the law requires it, and never after you opt out in the US states where you have that right.

Who we are

Apex Overdrive: 3D Racing (the "Game", package com.redpenstudio.apexoverdrive) is developed and published by Red Pen Studio ("we", "us").

Information we collect

Red Pen Studio does not collect personal information. The Game does not:

  • require a sign-up, name, email address or phone number (the cloud save uses an anonymous ID created automatically);
  • access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location;
  • use analytics or crash reporting SDKs, apart from the Meta install measurement described below;
  • send anything to the cloud other than the game progress listed under Cloud save below.

Three Google services and one Meta service built into the Game process data, under their own policies:

Cloud save (Google Firebase)

So your progress is not lost, the Game backs it up with Google Firebase (Google LLC), using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore in a Firebase project operated by Red Pen Studio:

  • When the Game first goes online, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous player ID for this installation. It is a random identifier and is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Under that ID, Cloud Firestore stores your game progress: coin balance, unlocked cars and paints, each car's upgrades, paint and rims, selected car, career progress (unlocked stage, stars per stage, best times and scores, claimed milestone rewards), which Play products are owned and have paid their coin bonus, the reward-crate timer, and the time and game version of the save.
  • Progress is uploaded only at a few moments (when you return to the garage after a race, after a purchase, and at launch when the cloud copy is checked), and only if something changed. Settings stay on your device.
  • Firebase receives the technical data needed to provide the service, such as your IP address and device and app information, as described in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Only the Game, signed in as your anonymous ID, can read or change your cloud save. If you are offline, the Game keeps working and uploads later. The cloud save is not used for advertising or analytics.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows a banner ad only on the Settings screen (never in the garage or during a race), a full-screen ad between races (never during your first three races, then after every two or three races and at most once every three minutes), and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and app-set ID;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language, app version and IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred);
  • ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks and video completion.

Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying the No Ads Pack or the Supercar Bundle turns off the banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded videos remain available only when you choose to watch one.

Install measurement (Meta)

We advertise the Game on Meta's apps (such as Facebook and Instagram). To learn how well those ads work, the Game includes the Meta SDK (Meta Platforms, Inc.; for people in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Meta Platforms Ireland Limited), used only for app-install and app-open measurement. It is not used for Facebook Login, sharing or any other Meta feature, and it does not show ads.

What is collected. When it is allowed to run, the Meta SDK sends Meta:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID), unless you have deleted it or opted out in Android settings, and an app-specific random ID the SDK creates;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size, app name and version, Meta SDK version, install referrer information from Google Play and your IP address;
  • install and app-open events: when the Game is installed and opened, and how long a session lasted;
  • technical diagnostics about the Meta SDK itself if it runs into an error.

The Game does not send Meta your name, email, game progress, purchases or any custom event, and screen titles and other extra app details are turned off.

Why. To measure the performance of our ads on Meta: which installs and app opens came from those ads, so we can decide where to advertise. Meta processes this data under its own terms and policy, and may also use it to provide and improve its ad measurement services.

Legal basis and consent in Europe. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we use the Meta SDK only with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules on device storage). The consent message shown at launch lists Meta as an ad partner; unless you consent to Meta, to storing and accessing information on your device and to measuring ad performance, the Meta SDK is not started and Meta receives nothing from the Game.

US state privacy choices. In US states with privacy laws that give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising, the Game shows Google's US state privacy message, and the Meta SDK runs only if you have chosen to allow it there. If you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information or of targeted advertising, if no choice is recorded yet, or if your choice cannot be read, the Meta SDK is not started and Meta receives nothing from the Game. A Global Privacy Control signal recorded in that message is treated as an opt-out.

Outside these regions, where neither consent nor a US state opt-out applies, the Meta SDK runs by default.

How to opt out. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and the US states covered above, change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy in the Game; the change applies immediately and the Meta SDK is not started again unless your choice allows it. Everywhere, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads, and you can review and disconnect activity that apps share with Meta in your Meta account under Your activity off Meta technologies. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection. Learn more in the Meta Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional one-time purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never receive your card or payment information. The Game receives only a purchase confirmation (product, purchase token and status) from Google Play on your device, which it uses to unlock what you bought and to restore it with Settings → Restore purchases. Google's processing of your payment is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your coin balance, unlocked cars and paints, each car's upgrades and customization, career progress, settings, purchase status and reward-crate timer locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. The progress part of it is also backed up to the cloud save described above; settings never leave your device unless your Android device's own backup service is enabled, in which case it is handled by Google under its privacy policy. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the Game is not designed to show ads to them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Game, or has provided personal information through it, contact us and we will help remove it; you can also reset the device's advertising ID in Android settings.

Data retention and security

Your cloud save is kept for as long as the Game's Firebase project runs, or until you ask us to delete it. It is protected by Firebase security rules that only let your own anonymous ID read or write it, and is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is retained and secured by Google under its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Data collected by the Meta SDK is retained and secured by Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites

The settings screen links to this policy and our Terms of Service, which open in your web browser. Websites are governed by their own privacy practices.

Your rights

To have your cloud save deleted, send us the Player ID shown at the bottom of the Game's Settings screen and we will delete it. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it (Play purchases stay on your Google account and can be restored). To manage data held by Google, including ad personalization, use your Google Account settings or Settings → Ad privacy in the Game. To manage data held by Meta, use Settings → Ad privacy in the Game (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws), your Meta account settings, or the options in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.

Contact
