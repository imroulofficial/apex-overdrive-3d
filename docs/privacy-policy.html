Privacy Policy

Apex Overdrive: 3D Racing · Red Pen Studio · Effective October 20, 2026

Summary: Apex Overdrive plays fully offline. The current version does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Your progress is saved only on your device.

Who we are

Apex Overdrive: 3D Racing (the "Game", package com.redpenstudio.apexoverdrive) is developed and published by Red Pen Studio ("we", "us").

Information we collect

The current version of the Game does not collect personal information. Specifically, it does not:

  • require an account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number;
  • access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location;
  • use analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • send gameplay data to any server operated by us or by third parties.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your coin balance, unlocked paints, upgrades and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device unless your Android device's own backup service is enabled, in which case it is handled by Google under its privacy policy. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Future features

Later versions may add optional in-app purchases (processed by Google Play) or advertising. Before any such feature collects or shares data, we will update this policy, the Google Play Data safety section, and request any consent required by law.

Links to other sites

The settings screen links to this policy and our Terms of Service, which open in your web browser. Websites are governed by their own privacy practices.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Red Pen Studio at the developer contact email shown on the Game's Google Play listing.
