Privacy Policy

Apex Overdrive: 3D Racing · Red Pen Studio · Effective October 21, 2026

Summary: Apex Overdrive plays fully offline and Red Pen Studio does not run any servers that receive your data. Your progress is saved only on your device. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects device data (including your advertising ID) as described below. Optional purchases are processed by Google Play.

Who we are

Apex Overdrive: 3D Racing (the "Game", package com.redpenstudio.apexoverdrive) is developed and published by Red Pen Studio ("we", "us").

Information we collect

Red Pen Studio does not collect personal information. The Game does not:

  • require an account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number;
  • access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location;
  • use analytics or crash reporting SDKs;
  • send gameplay data to any server operated by us.

Two Google services built into the Game do process data, under Google's own policies:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows a banner ad in the garage, a full-screen ad after every few races, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and app-set ID;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language, app version and IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred);
  • ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks and video completion.

Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying Remove Ads or the Supercar Bundle turns off the banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded videos remain available only when you choose to watch one.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional one-time purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never receive your card or payment information. The Game receives only a purchase confirmation (product, purchase token and status) from Google Play on your device, which it uses to unlock what you bought and to restore it with Settings → Restore purchases. Google's processing of your payment is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your coin balance, unlocked cars and paints, upgrades, settings, purchase status and reward-crate timer locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device unless your Android device's own backup service is enabled, in which case it is handled by Google under its privacy policy. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the Game is not designed to show ads to them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Game, or has provided personal information through it, contact us and we will help remove it; you can also reset the device's advertising ID in Android settings.

Data retention and security

We do not hold any of your data. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is retained and secured by Google under its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Links to other sites

The settings screen links to this policy and our Terms of Service, which open in your web browser. Websites are governed by their own privacy practices.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it (Play purchases stay on your Google account and can be restored). To manage data held by Google, including ad personalization, use your Google Account settings or Settings → Ad privacy in the Game.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Red Pen Studio at the developer contact email shown on the Game's Google Play listing.
